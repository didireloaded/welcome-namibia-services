# Conversation handover for Codex

## Client's idea

A business expected to start in 2027, helping foreigners with work permits, study permits and visas, airport pick-ups, medical travel paperwork and practical assistance, vacations and hotel reservations. A meeting and commercial quote still need to be agreed.

## User's direction

Start the website before the client meeting. Research comparable agencies and identify useful improvements. Build the UI first. Use all sections of the attached visual reference and the provided technical notes. The supplied design is the approved visual direction for this build.

## Research already done

- NamVisas publishes agency service fees, excludes government and other charges, and offers airport meet-and-assist and accommodation sourcing. https://namvisas.com/services
- ProAvia combines visa support with transfers and accommodation and uses WhatsApp enquiries. https://proaviainc.com/services/visa-handling
- VisaNam lists immigration services, document storage and renewal reminders. https://www.visanam.com/
- Descubra Worldwide serves Namibia and Angola with education, accommodation and medical travel services. https://descubraworldwide.com/

The proposed differentiation is a clear joined-up visitor journey from preparation through arrival and the stay. It is not a claim that competitors lack these services offline.

## Working identity

Arrival Namibia is a placeholder, not the confirmed business name. The source is designed to be rebranded once the client supplies the identity.

## Supplied design

`ui-reference.png` contains the photographic tour hero, pill glass navigation, hero thumbnail carousel, tabbed service gallery, customer/story image area, dark geometric package cards, partner/trust strip and four-step booking layout. All major visual sections are represented in the homepage. Medical, study, work and holiday copy replaces the original Mount Bromo tour copy.

## Current deliverable

A responsive frontend, request demos, client portal and admin portal. Database, authentication, secure uploads, payment processing and communication integrations remain backend work. Testimonial and registration information require real client-supplied evidence. Preparation checklists are illustrative and not a definitive immigration requirement list.

## Scope decisions to confirm with client

Business name, domain, brand assets, countries served, inbound versus outbound travel, exact service boundaries, direct versus partner services, languages, contact details, fee structure, response hours, cancellation terms, registration, medical provider relationships and payment provider.

## Official resources

- Ministry e-services: https://eservices.mhaiss.gov.na/
- Namibia Tourism Board registration forms: https://visitnamibia.com.na/registration-forms/

Visa and permit decisions stay with the responsible authority. Medical advice and treatment stay with qualified providers. Do not promise expedited approvals, clinical escorts, 24-hour availability or partner relationships without confirmation.
